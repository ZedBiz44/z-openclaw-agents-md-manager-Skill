## Required Decision Notes

For every `Relocate` item, record why the destination is loaded or discoverable when needed.

For every `Retire` item, record the duplicate, replacement, obsolete runtime behavior, or approval that makes removal safe.

For every compressed or merged safety rule, state how the new wording preserves the original authority and stop condition.

## Register Gate

- Every material instruction is listed.
- Every row has one decision.
- Every relocation has a verified destination.
- Every retirement has a reason.
- Every critical rule has a behavior test.
- No item is removed only to meet the size target.
